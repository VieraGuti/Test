## OpenShorts

Project: https://github.com/mutonby/openshorts

Our thumbnail workflow adapts the *approach* of selecting diverse frames,
ranking them by visual clarity, and adding accurate text without inventing
video events. We do not import its full backend, face detection model, GPU
pipeline, or hosted service.

## Noto Sans

The lightweight thumbnail worker may fetch Noto Sans from
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans
when fonts are not installed on the machine. Its font files are licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 by the Noto Project Authors. The font is cached
at runtime, not committed to this repository. This file does not redistribute
